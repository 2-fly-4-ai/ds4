The fog had been thick for three days, a gray wool blanket that smothered the world and left Elias alone with the rhythmic *thrum-thrum-thrum* of the Fresnel lens. He was seventy-two, his hands rough as barnacles, his life measured in rotations of glass and the smell of kerosene.

It was Tuesday when the letter arrived.
